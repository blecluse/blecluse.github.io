Documents

I have gathered on this page some documents written during my studies.

Directed Reading L3

Directed reading project carried out in collaboration with Samuel Chan-Ashing, under the supervision of Jérémy Martin. We focused on uncertainty principles and the Logvinenko-Sereda theorem.

L3 Internship Report

I completed my L3 internship at Aix-Marseille University under the supervision of Sary Drappeau, working on probabilistic number theory. In particular, I proved the Erdös-Kac theorem: a normal distribution hides within the distribution of prime numbers.

M1 Internship Report

I completed my M1 internship at the University of Edinburgh, Scotland, under the supervision of Jonathan Hickman. My project focused on the fundamentals of harmonic analysis and the study of maximal functions.
