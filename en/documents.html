Documents

This page contains a selection of documents I wrote during my studies.

Third-year undergraduate reading project

Reading project carried out in collaboration with Samuel Chan-Ashing, under the supervision of Jérémy Martin. We studied uncertainty principles and the Logvinenko–Sereda theorem.

Third-year undergraduate internship report

I completed my third-year undergraduate internship at Aix-Marseille University under the supervision of Sary Drappeau, in probabilistic number theory. In particular, I studied the Erdős–Kac theorem, which reveals a Gaussian law in the distribution of the number of prime factors of integers.

First-year master’s internship report

I completed my first-year master’s internship at the University of Edinburgh, Scotland, under the supervision of Jonathan Hickman. My project focused on the foundations of harmonic analysis and the study of maximal functions.

Master’s thesis – agrégation preparation

Master’s thesis written during my preparation for the French agrégation examination. It focuses on topic 142: Greatest common divisors and least common multiples, computational algorithms and applications.

Second-year master’s thesis

I completed my second-year master’s internship at the Laboratoire de Mathématiques d’Orsay under the supervision of Laurent Moonens. We studied the theory of differentiation of integrals and maximal operators.
